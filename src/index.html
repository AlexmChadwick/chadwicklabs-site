  1. 01

    Petition Checker

    Status: Live prototype

    Helps people in Indiana fill out and check a protection order petition (form PO-0100) before they file it, right in the browser.

    • Team project for Northeastern's AI for Impact course.
    • Not official and not legal advice.
    • po-checker.chadwicklabs.com↗

  3. 03

    VMBridge for Parallels Desktop

    Status: Shipped, v1.0.2 for macOS

    Lets Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor drive a Parallels Desktop VM (screen, keyboard, mouse, commands, files) while your Mac desktop stays out of it.

    • macOS 14+, Parallels Desktop Pro or Business.
    • Parallels is a registered trademark of Parallels International GmbH. VMBridge is not affiliated with or endorsed by Parallels.
    • vmbridge.app↗
    • Launch post on X↗

  5. 05

    Cairn

    Status: In development, not available yet

    A macOS menu bar app, with an iPhone companion, that shows how much of your AI tool usage limits you have left at a glance.

    • Built on the open-source OpenUsage project by Robin Ebers (MIT).

  6. 06

    Autorouter

    Status: In development

    A task router for AI agents. Bots drop jobs in one shared queue, and it sends each one to whichever coding model or local GPU is actually free, rerouting when one hits its limit or goes offline.

  7. 07

    Toontown

    Status: In development

    A cross-platform Toontown fork built on Open Toontown, targeting 3DS, iPhone and iPad, Android, Mac, Windows and the web.

    • In development. Builds in testing on several targets, nothing released yet.

  8. 08

    Clamshell

    Status: In development

    Clamshell streams 3DS games from the Azahar emulator to your iPhone over local Wi-Fi, and sends touch and controller input back.
